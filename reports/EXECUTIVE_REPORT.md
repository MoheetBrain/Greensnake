> **This is a discovery instrument, not a recommendation engine.** Elite overlap is an information signal about where unusually well-informed people are looking. It says nothing on its own about valuation, dilution, access, liquidity, unit economics, burn, competition, technical feasibility, regulation or downside. Anything surfaced here still requires independent diligence.

> **The backtest does NOT yet show that elite overlap predicts outcomes, and the separation it reports must not be believed.** Only a handful of cohort companies on either arm have overlap that was *knowable* at the historical date, and the failure arm is the thinner of the two — because collection followed people, and people's portfolios are documented through the companies that worked. That is survivorship bias operating on the evidence itself. This graph can say where elite capital is concentrated today; it cannot yet say whether that concentration predicts anything. See [question 15](SECONDARY_QUESTIONS.md).
