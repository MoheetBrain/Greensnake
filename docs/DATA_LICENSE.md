## Third-party exclusions

Underlying articles, portfolio pages, regulatory filings, market-data services, source titles, attributed quotations, trademarks and other third-party material are not relicensed. Their original rights, source terms and any applicable database rights remain in force. A public URL or paid access is not a redistribution license. Reusers must assess rights for their own intended use of third-party material.

Sources are retained as URLs, publisher/tier metadata and concise evidence summaries; this repository does not bundle full source articles or a vendor database dump. The collection contract requires original summaries rather than long copyrighted passages. Notes about inaccessible or paywalled sources are preserved, along with unresolved and rejected claims. A PitchBook newsletter citation is a short attributed valuation summary, not a proprietary dataset export.

The publication review identified no bundled restricted source dataset or full copyrighted publication. That review is an inspection of the repository and its provenance notes, not a representation that third-party services have granted blanket reuse rights. The licensing grant is limited accordingly. The historical author-metadata blocker was resolved by an independent public snapshot; original Git history remains private. See [publication notes](PUBLICATION_NOTES.md#historical-privacy-blocker-and-resolution).
